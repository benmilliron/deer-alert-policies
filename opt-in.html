Deer Alert SMS Opt-In

Deer Alert is a self-hosted deer-detection application that sends automated SMS notifications when a deer is detected by the camera system.

How to Opt In

To receive automated Deer Alert SMS notifications, text START to +114195404679.

By texting START, you expressly consent to receive automated text messages from Deer Alert when the system detects a deer.

Message frequency varies based on deer activity. Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help.
