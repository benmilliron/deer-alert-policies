Milliron_Home SMS Opt-In

Milliron_Home is a self-hosted deer-detection application that sends automated SMS notifications when a deer is detected by the camera system.

How to Opt In

To receive automated Milliron_Home SMS notifications, text START to +114195404679.

By texting START, you expressly consent to receive automated text messages from Milliron_Home when the system detects a deer.

Message frequency varies based on deer activity. Message and data rates may apply.
